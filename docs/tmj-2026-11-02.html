📋 今日文獻趨勢

Today's research highlights a shift toward understanding TMD through biopsychosocial lenses, with significant findings on adherence to conservative therapy, the role of personality and somatization in symptom expression, and the development of more efficient diagnostic criteria. Digital health and self-management interventions show promise but require higher quality evidence. There is also growing interest in advanced imaging and AI for objective TMJ assessment, alongside investigations into oxidative stress and molecular mechanisms like macrophage-neuron crosstalk.
